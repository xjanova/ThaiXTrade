§2 · Problem Statement

Four barriers between blockchain and everyday commerce

01
High Gas Fees
Ethereum charges $5–50 per transaction; BSC up to $1.00. Micro-payments are priced out entirely.
02
Complexity Barrier
DeFi interfaces assume technical fluency, excluding the 95% who have never held a crypto asset.
03
No ASEAN Focus
Major ecosystems target Western markets — no Thai-language rails, no local payment integration.
04
Fragmented Utility
Most tokens are speculative instruments with no link to agriculture, logistics, or retail.

§2 · TPIX Solution

Each barrier answered at the protocol level

01
Zero Gas Fees
Gas price fixed at 0 in genesis. Every transfer, swap and contract call is free, permanently.
02
Intuitive UX
Connect, swap, stake — three clicks, in Thai or English, with no gas configuration to understand.
03
ASEAN-First Design
Full Thai localisation, local payment rails and use cases drawn from regional commerce.
04
Real-World Integration
FoodPassport, IoT smart farming, delivery, e-commerce and hotel booking all settle in TPIX.
