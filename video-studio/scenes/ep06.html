§9 · The Structural Obstacle

Everywhere in marketing. Almost nowhere in practice.

Barrier 01
Cost
A strategy that evaluates the market every minute pays gas on every evaluation — and again on every rebalance.
Worst case — spread arbitrage
Fig. 1 — The strategy is dead before the first trade.
Barrier 02
Conflict of Interest
A centralised exchange is the counterparty to its own users. It holds the deposits. It sees the order book. Its revenue rises when its users lose.
Fig. 2 — However good the code, the incentive points the wrong way.

§9 · Scope & Position
Stated plainly — what the engine does not do
The user selects the trading pair. The engine decides buy, sell or hold on that pair. It does not scan the market and choose an asset.
Automatic pair selection is on the roadmap — not in the build.
